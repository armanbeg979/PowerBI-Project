Power BI – Welcome to my Power BI project. This project helped me explore how data can tell real stories — in this case, understanding how people make investment decisions.

🌟 About Cognifyz Technologies: Cognifyz Technologies is a company that works in data science, AI, and analytics. They provide smart solutions and also offer internships that help students learn practical data skills.


🧩 Project Overview: Here’s a short summary of what each task was about 👇


Task 1: Data Exploration and Summary

Imported and cleaned the dataset in Power BI.
Found out the average age, total respondents, total investments, and investment patterns.
Visualized popular savings objectives like Retirement Plan, Health Care, and Education.

Task 2: Gender-Based Analysis

Compared how males and females invest differently.
Males preferred Equity Markets, while females showed interest in Gold and Fixed Deposits.


Task 3: Objective Analysis

Looked at how people’s investment objectives (like Growth or Income) are linked to the types of investments they choose.
Found that those focused on Capital Appreciation preferred Equity and PPF.


Task 4: Investment Duration and Frequency

Analyzed how long people keep their investments and how often they monitor them.
Most investors preferred a 3–5 year duration and checked their portfolios monthly.


Task 5: Reasons for Investment

Explored why people invest.
Top reasons were Capital Appreciation, Assured Returns, and Better Returns.


Task 6: Source of Information

Studied where investors get their financial information from.
Financial Consultants and Newspapers/Magazines were the most trusted sources.


Task 7: Combined Dashboard

Brought together all six tasks into one interactive Power BI dashboard.
Added filters, slicers, and summary metrics to make it easy to explore.
Presented final insights clearly and professionally.


📊 Key Insights

Here are the main findings from my overall analysis:

The average investor age is around 27.8 years.
PPF and Fixed Deposits are the most popular investments.
Most investors hold their investments for 3–5 years and check them monthly.
The main goal of investing is Retirement Planning.
People mostly invest for Capital Appreciation and Better Returns.
Financial Consultants are the most common source of investment advice.

💼 Business Decisions & Recommendations:

Based on the insights from the analysis, here are some data-driven business decisions and suggestions that could be made:

Target Younger Investors (Aged 25–30):
Marketing campaigns can focus on this age group as they form the majority of investors and are more open to financial planning.
Promote Medium-Term Investment Products (3–5 Years):
Since most investors prefer medium-term durations, businesses can introduce flexible plans or schemes with 3–5 year maturity options.

Enhance Monthly Engagement Tools:
As investors monitor their portfolios monthly, financial firms can design monthly newsletters, dashboards, or app notifications to increase engagement.
Leverage Financial Consultants and Media Partnerships:
Partnering with financial consultants, newspapers, and magazines can strengthen brand trust and reach, as these are key information sources.

Personalized Advisory Services:
Offer personalized investment advice based on gender and objective insights — for example, suggesting equity plans for growth-focused males and gold or deposits for security-oriented female investors.
Educate Investors on Diversification:
As PPF and Fixed Deposits dominate, companies can educate clients about diversification and promote mutual funds and equities for better long-term returns.

Product Development:
Develop Capital Appreciation-focused investment products as this is the most common investment motive among respondents.
These recommendations show how Power BI insights can directly influence marketing, product design, and client engagement strategies.
